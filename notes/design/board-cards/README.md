# Board cards — three levels

Captures of the dashboard's board, on the demo board (`scripts/demo.py`), in
French, at 1440×900 and 390×844, light and dark — the board scrolled to its
columns (a phone draws one column, *Bloqué*).

- `before/` — `main` before the change: every fact at the same level, the
  priority as a badge going red, model and goal as chips, three buttons, the
  bottom line in mono. Three cards per column at 1440×900, four on a phone.
- `after/` — the title alone (two lines at most); the state and the one filled
  button (*Lancer*, *Débloquer*, *Décider*, *Pause*; *Lancer maintenant* stays
  an outline, a ready ticket starts on its own), the other gestures behind *…*;
  one quiet line with the sub-type, the project, the age and the cost. The
  priority is the thin bar along the left edge, neutral to orange. Five cards
  per column at 1440×900, five on a phone.
