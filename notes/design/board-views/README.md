# Board views — Board | List | Agenda

Captures of the dashboard's view switcher, on the demo board (`scripts/demo.py`),
in English and French, at 1440×900 and 390×844.

- `before/` — `main` before the change: « Tableau | Liste » in French, two views.
- `after/` — « Board | List | Agenda »:
  - `board-*` — the switcher, *Board* in every language;
  - `table-fields-*` — the List with its *Shown fields* menu open;
  - `calendar-*` — the Agenda in its first view (the week; the month on a phone), scrolled to two scheduled tickets;
  - `calendar-month-*` — the month: tickets in the console's orange, the recurring tickets' coming launches in violet with ↻.
